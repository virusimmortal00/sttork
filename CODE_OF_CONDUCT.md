# Community Code of Conduct

Zork Voice welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities,
experience levels, and ways of communicating.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Make room for accessibility needs and unfamiliarity with the codebase.
- Respect privacy, spoiler preferences, and the rights of original creators.
- Stop behavior when someone clearly asks you to stop.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private reports or
credentials are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers using the private contact mechanism on
their GitHub profiles. Do not post identifying details in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban a participant when
needed to protect the community. They should preserve confidentiality, avoid
conflicts of interest, and explain an enforcement decision to the affected
people when doing so is safe.
